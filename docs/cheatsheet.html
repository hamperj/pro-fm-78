The panel

Algorithm
Picks the type: 1978, Bass, Lead, Pad and so on. It jumps to the program you last played in that type.
Presets Select
Steps through the programs within the type. Both wrap around. The screen shows "3 OF 21".
Knob 1–4
Set the four controls on the current page, left to right. Turn quickly to move four steps per click.
FX SEL ENV LFO EDIT
Open a section. Pressing the same button again goes to its next page, and after its last page you're back on HOME. The lit button is the current section; the others glow.
ARP SEQ
Open the arpeggiator and loop recorder pages, the same way. ARP stays lit while the arp is on; SEQ while the loop plays.
Play
Start or stop the loop.
Rec
Tap while stopped to arm (it flashes): your first note starts the loop and recording. Tap while playing to record over the loop, and again to stop recording. Hold 1 s to clear the loop: HOLD TO CLEAR and a red bar fill while you hold, and LOOP CLEARED shows when it's done. Letting go after that does nothing.
Save
Store the loop and the chord, arp and loop settings in flash, loaded each time PRO-FM-78 starts. Stop the loop and let the notes fade first: otherwise it shows STOP THE LOOP FIRST or LET NOTES END, since saving pauses the sound for about 0.1 s.
Home
Tap for PRO-FM-78's home screen. Hold 1.5 s to return to the FM-1 host.
Oct − Oct +
Shift the keys ±2 octaves. The top line shows OCT +1, plus ARP and the loop's state: ARMED, REC 2.3 or PLAY 1.4 (bar.beat). With no shift, the lowest key is F3.
Keys
Play notes at velocity 100, or at the sensed velocity when the key reports one. Key pressure goes to aftertouch.

Finding sounds

All 200 factory programs, sorted into types by name. The ALGORITHM knob goes through them in this order:

  • 1978 40
  • BASS 13
  • LEAD 21
  • POLY 21
  • PAD 29
  • STRINGS 8
  • BRASS 9
  • WIND 6
  • KEYS 14
  • ORGAN 11
  • PLUCK 13
  • BELLS 17
  • PERC 8
  • FX 30
  • INIT 1
  • 1978 holds the 40 original programs from 1978 (group 5). PRO-FM-78 starts here.
  • The red LEDs read group · bank · program, as on the original panel.
  • The other types draw on all 200 programs, so 1978 sounds appear there too.
  • INIT is a plain starting patch for building your own.
  • EDITED shows once you've moved a sound control.

Sound edits aren't saved: changing program or switching module reloads the factory version. SAVE stores only the loop and the chord, arp and loop settings.

Chord generator

On the SEL page. Each key or MIDI note plays the whole chord, and releasing it releases the chord, even if you changed the chord while holding. The chord's name shows in orange on the program display. The setting stays when you change program.

Invert
Moves the lowest notes up an octave, one per step. A step past the chord's size wraps to ROOT (3RD needs a four-note chord).
Voicing
CLOSE as played. OPEN lifts the second note an octave. +BASS adds the root an octave below.
Strum
Plays the notes one after another, 10 ms apart per step.

Arpeggiator

Notes go keys → chord generator → arp → voices, so one key with a chord set gives a chord arpeggio. The first note starts the arp on the beat.

  • UP / DOWN / UP/DN go low to high (or back) across the notes held, then up each extra octave. PLAYED keeps the order you pressed them.
  • Latch keeps the notes going after you let go. Your next press after letting go of everything starts a new set.
  • The keys light up as the arp plays their notes.

Loop recorder

  • Records in real time into a loop of 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars. Keys snap to the nearest 1/16.
  • With the arp on, the arp's notes are recorded as they play instead of the keys.
  • Playback goes through the chord generator (for recorded keys) but not the arp. Transp shifts the whole loop.
  • Click: REC clicks while recording, ON whenever the loop plays. PLAY with an empty loop gives you a metronome.
  • The loop and settings stay when you change program, so you can audition sounds over it. Up to 256 notes.

Playing

  • Mod wheel adds LFO modulation on top of the LFO amount. The LFO page's Noise knob mixes in noise.
  • Aftertouch adds LFO depth and/or opens the filter, if the program has pressure enabled.
  • Pitch bend range is set by each program, 1–12 semitones.
  • Voices: 8 when both cores are running, 5 on one core. Unison programs stack every voice on one note.
